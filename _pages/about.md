Hi—I'm **Rowen Glusman**, an MSc student in **Astronomy & Astrophysics (UvA)** with hands-on experience in **exoplanet occurrence rates, transit modeling, and data-intensive inference**. I love to tackle data analysis problems in stellar and planetary astrophysics: building robust pipelines, extracting interesting signals from noisy light curves, and turning messy datasets into publishable results.

I’m actively looking for **PhD opportunities** and **research collaborations**. Let's chat!

---

## Research focus
- **Machine learning** — utilizing machine learning to improve the search for particular time-series signals such as exoplanet and eclipsing binary transits.  
- **Time-series inference** — transit fitting (Batman), feature engineering beyond auto-extractors, and ODR/likelihood-based model comparison.  
- **Reproducible pipelines** — Python-first workflows, versioned data products, and benchmarking across surveys.

## What I’m working on now
- Cross-comparing exoplanet occurrence rate datasets with consistent mass/period fit ranges.  
- Generating interpretable **feature sets** for light curves and **parametric transit fits** to capture depth, duration, and χ²_red for downstream classifiers.  
- Using **machine learning** techniques to further vet exoplanet and EB candidates prior to manual vetting.
