I have several hobbies which I pursue in my somewhat limited downtime. Chances are, we share at least one!

- **Music**: I enjoy both playing and listening to music. If there's an empty Saturday, chances are you'll find me attending a concert or messing around with my own instruments.
- **Dancing**: I like to dance socially, both in Lindy Hop and blues. I also like to take classes in a few other styles. If you're a true internet sleuth, you might be able to find my college dance group's YouTube channel.
- **Baking**: You may have to physically stop me from bringing banana bread to group meeting.
- **Gaming**: I'm quite attached to Civilization IV, which is vastly superior to any other game in the series. I'm also a big fan of visually beautiful, story-oriented puzzle games like the Myst series or Journey.
- **Knitting**: I once knitted an octopus-shaped tea cosy for my Dad's birthday. 'Nuff said, I think.

I'm always on the hunt for a new hobby. Let me know if you have a suggestion!
